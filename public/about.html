About

We are Higor and Janaína Cazuza, building our careers in software engineering through Java backend development, system design, and real-world engineering practice.

Asymptora is where we turn that pursuit into actual work: studying the foundations, building systems, making engineering decisions, and documenting what we learn along the way.

Java is the foundation.

Java is at the center of our engineering journey. We use it to go beyond writing code: to understand domain modeling, software design, architecture, data, consistency, testing, failure, and the trade-offs that shape real systems.

The goal is not simply to learn a language. It is to become software engineers who can reason about the systems they build.

Engineering work, not demonstrations.

Asymptora is built around the idea that software engineering should be supported by evidence. We document requirements and decisions, test what we build, investigate failures, operate real systems, and keep a record of how the work evolves.

What matters is not presenting something that looks finished. What matters is understanding why it works, how it behaves, and what happens when it doesn't.

Built by two engineers.

Asymptora is the shared engineering journey of Janaína and Higor. Different backgrounds, a common transition into software engineering, and a shared commitment to building the knowledge and experience required to work with real systems.

An engineering lab.

Asymptora is an engineering lab: a space for practicing software engineering in the open, not a company with a product to sell or work that competes with anyone's employment. It is open source, and we intend to keep it active for as long as we keep learning, which is to say, for as long as we do this.
