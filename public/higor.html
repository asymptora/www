Higor

Java backend engineer, with a focus on the infrastructure that runs what he builds.

Higor is a Java backend engineer drawn to systems that show symptoms before they reveal their causes. When something doesn't behave as it should, he wants to understand why, not just make the problem disappear.

He built that way of working in criminal law. Analyzing a case meant reconstructing what happened from the available evidence, separating facts from accounts, finding the flaw in an argument, and building a defense that could withstand scrutiny, under deadlines that left no room for error. That is where he developed the critical reasoning, complex problem solving, and clear communication he now brings to engineering: explaining a decision to the people who need to trust it is part of the work, not an afterthought.

Java is where he deepens that practice: domain modeling, correctness, consistency, testing, and the design decisions that determine whether a system still holds when the conditions change. A working implementation does not end the problem for him. It is where the harder questions begin.

At Asymptora, that work centers on Asymptora Collect, the billing and receivables product he is responsible for, a domain he came to know closely through years of managing contracts and defaults.

His extension beyond the code is infrastructure. He operates the lab's own servers, where every decision is recorded with its reasoning and rejected alternatives, and every failure becomes documented knowledge. Understanding what runs a system is, for him, part of understanding the system itself.

His focus is Java backend engineering, grounded in the infrastructure that sustains it.
