Products

Two individual products, and the platform that will eventually connect them. All three exist as specifications right now: the engineering that builds them hasn't started yet.

Asymptora Collect (Higor)

Billing and receivables: issuing charges, tracking payments, reconciling what came in against what was owed. In specification.

Asymptora Balance (Janaína)

Personal finance and budgeting: accounts, categorization, budgets, and making sense of where money actually goes. In specification.

Asymptora Pay (joint)

The platform that connects both products: internal transfers, shared infrastructure for payments between them. Planned, not yet started.
